A few words

Where creativity and technology meet

I grew up near Salt Lake City with a camera in my hand, then moved to Montana as a teenager and fell in love with hiking, fishing, archery and riding horses. After earning a technology degree from the University of Montana, I moved to Fargo, North Dakota, and built a career in tech.

To balance all that screen time, I took up scrapbooking. That turned into co-founding the online ScrapbookingClasses group, which grew to over 1,500 members, where I designed and taught new layouts every week.

Today I'm combining both sides: years of tech experience and a love of teaching. If technology or AI feels overwhelming, I'd love to help you take the next step.
